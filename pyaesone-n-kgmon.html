About the characters

Pyae Sone, apolite boy, who loves to read. He loves to teach good manners and cultural etiquette to Kaung Mon. A Burmese cat, who loves to wear Shan traditional outfit. He is mischievous, naughty and playful cat. But he loves Pyae Sone and he loves to stay beside Pyae Sone.
