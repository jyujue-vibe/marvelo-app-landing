For all the animals who inspire us to explore, learn, and care for our world. Every animal has a story worth telling. Dedicated to all the amazing animals who share our beautiful world with us.

TINY BALA BAT

JIT JIT & MEL LONE
TRAVEL ADVENTURES
Page 1

"Mel Lone, we're going to meet another animal with a special nose, just like you!"

Mel Lone touched his snub nose.

"Really? Who?"

"The Bala Tube-nosed Bat in Thailand! They have tube-shaped noses, and they're in danger"

Page 2

The next morning, Jit Jit and Mel Lone arrived in the beautiful Bala Forest of Thailand.

"It's so green and misty!" whispered Mel Lone.

"Let's find the bat!"

Page 3

"Bats come out at night," said Jit Jit.

"We need to wait until evening."

As the sky turned pink and orange, the forest came alive with sounds. Crickets chirped and frogs croaked.

Page 4

As darkness fell, a tiny bat landed on a branch right in front of them!

"Hello! I'm Betty the Bala Bat!" she said with a sweet voice.

"You can talk!" gasped Jit Jit.

"And you're so tiny!"

Page 5

"I'm very small—I weigh less than a cookie and can fit in your hand!" said Betty proudly.

Mel Lone's eyes went wide.

"You're even smaller than me!"

"Much smaller!" giggled Betty.

Page 6

"Mel Lone, you have a snub nose, and I have tube-shaped nostrils!" said Betty.

"Look—they stick out like tiny straws! My tubes give me a supercharged sense of smell! I can sniff out food with amazing accuracy!"

Mel Lone touched his nose.

"We both have special noses!"

Page 7

"My fur looks gray," said Betty, "but look closely!"

She spun around, and her fur sparkled!

"I have silver-gold guard hairs that shimmer in the moonlight!"

Page 8

"I eat insects, but I can be very fussy! Some bats like me who live with humans are picky eaters!"

"What do you mean?" asked Jit Jit.

"One day I might love bananas, but the next day I might want something different" Betty giggled.

Page 9

Betty suddenly flew toward some bugs.

"Watch this! I'm a great insect hunter!" She caught a mosquito and came back.

"I eat lots of bugs—yummy!"

Page 10

"How do you catch bugs in the dark?" wondered Jit Jit.

"I use echolocation! I make special sounds that bounce back and tell me where things are!" said Betty proudly.

"Like a superpower!" said Mel Lone.

Page 11

"Watch this trick!" said Betty. She hung from a leaf and wrapped her wings around herself. Then she rocked back and forth gently!

"When I move like this in the wind, I look just like a leaf! Predators can't find me!"

Page 12

Betty stretched out her wings.

"See the yellow spots on my wings? They help me hide in the dappled forest light!"

"Like polka dots!" said Mel Lone.

"So pretty!"

Page 13

"Come meet my family!" said Betty happily. More tiny bats appeared, flying all around! They swooped and danced in the moonlight.

"This is my home and my family," smiled Betty.

Page 14

Betty's voice became quiet.

"My whole family lives in this one small forest—less than 4 square kilometres." "That's so tiny!" said Jit Jit.

"Yes. If even a small part of this forest is destroyed, my entire species could disappear forever," said Betty sadly.

Page 15

"When people cut down trees, we lose our homes and have nowhere else to go," Betty explained.

"We only live here in Bala Forest, nowhere else in the whole world!"

Page 16

"Will you help us?" asked Betty hopefully.

"Tell people about Bala Bats so they'll protect our forest?"

"Yes!" said Jit Jit.

"I promise to tell everyone how special you are!" Mel Lone nodded.

"We'll help protect your home!"

Page 17

As they left the forest, Betty flew beside them one more time.

"Thank you for caring about us!" she called.

"We will never forget you, Betty!" Jit Jit called back.

"We'll help protect you and your family!"

Page 18

Back home, Jit Jit pulled out her special diary.

"I have so much to write about Betty and her family! It was the best adventure ever!"

Jit Jit smiled and opened to a fresh page.

"Betty asked us to help, and we will!"

Page 19

Today I met the most amazing animal - a Bala Tube-nosed Bat

- Betty and Mel Lone both have unique noses

- yellow wing spots help her blend in with dappled forest light.

Betty's family lives in a tiny area. When people cut down trees, they lose their only home. Even though their home is small, we can make a BIG difference by protecting it!

The End
